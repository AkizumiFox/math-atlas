    Economics and econometrics

    Least squares, definiteness and the second-derivative test, linear programming duality and matrix games, Markov chains, and the M-matrices behind an input-output model.

    It aims at 11.4 Least Squares and Minimum-Norm Solutions, 14.6 Definiteness and Second Derivatives, 18.6 Linear Programming Duality, 18.7 Matrix Games and the Minimax Theorem, 19.6 Markov Chains, 19.9 M-Matrices.

    105 sections to read, of 237. That is everything the proofs of those sections rest on, and nothing else.

    134 if you also work the exercises, since a written solution is the proof of its exercise: 29 more sections.
