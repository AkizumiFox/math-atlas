    Computer science and machine learning

    The singular value decomposition and what is built on it: low-rank approximation, the pseudoinverse, principal components, graph Laplacians and PageRank.

    It aims at 13.11 Low-Rank Approximation, 13.12 The Moore–Penrose Pseudoinverse, 19.8 PageRank, 24.10 Principal Components, 24.11 Graph Laplacians.

    98 sections to read, of 237. That is everything the proofs of those sections rest on, and nothing else.

    126 if you also work the exercises, since a written solution is the proof of its exercise: 28 more sections.
